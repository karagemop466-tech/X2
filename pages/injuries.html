Injuries & Depth-Chart Tracking

The system tracks injuries and identifies offensive/defensive deficiencies when starters are replaced by 2nd, 3rd, or 4th-string backups. Same model applies to offense and defense.

Official injury source: NFL.com official injury report — Week 10, 2026 (verified 2026-11-14). Depth-chart note: NFL.com does not publish a single official 1st–4th-string depth-chart page; replacement-string values from third-party aggregators are flagged unverified and must be confirmed on official NFL team pages (nfl.com/teams/{team}).

Deficiency rule (computed from official injury inputs)

A position group is FLAGGED as deficient when:

  • starter game status == "Out", OR
  • starter game status == "Questionable" with practice status == "Did Not Participate"

Severity scales with replacement string: 2nd string = moderate, 3rd string = high, 4th string = severe.

Offense vs. defense coverage

The model applies identically to both units:

  • Offense: QB, RB, WR, TE, OT, OG, C — a starter replaced by a backup shifts the offensive rating down.
  • Defense: EDGE, DT, LB, CB, S — a starter replaced by a backup shifts the defensive rating down.
  • Special teams: K, P tracked separately; minimal effect on moneyline/totals but logged.
Missing coverage: this capture is a Week 10 sample of games fetched from the official page; not all 32 teams are represented. Missing rows are left blank and flagged unverified on the Flagged for Review page.
