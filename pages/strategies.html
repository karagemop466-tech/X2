Strategies & Signals — originate our own NFL lines

Treat signal generation as a leaderboard competition that rewards top-performing strategies. Single bets, parlays, and player-prop combinations. Every strategy passes validation gates; correlation values are determined; luck is removed by construction.

Analysis — not official NFL data. Lines, signals, correlations, gates, and leaderboard rules are original work product. Team-level inputs (standings, injuries) use verified official NFL data and are linked on the Sources page.

1. Line origination (proposal)

Opening line = (away team rating − home team rating) + home-field advantage (~2.5 points, to be calibrated). Team rating built from verified official inputs:

  • Win rate W/(W+L+T) — NFL.com standings (verified)
  • Points differential (PF − PA) per game — NFL.com standings (verified)
  • Home / road / division / conference splits — NFL.com standings (verified)
  • Injury adjustments — starter Out/Questionable at high-impact positions shifts team rating down — NFL.com injury report (verified)

Our line vs. market line = edge. Bet when edge exceeds the G5 threshold (TBD). Market odds are not official — see Suggestion SUGG-003.

5. Leaderboard competition & rewards

  • Each admitted strategy is scored weekly on paper results: units won, win rate, ROI, edge accuracy.
  • Leaderboard published here and updated weekly after NFL results are official.
  • Rewards (proposal): top 3 strategies each month receive recognition + increased paper allocation.
  • No real-money allocation until gates G4–G6 pass for 8 consecutive weeks.

6. Removing luck — design principles

  1. Single bets first; parlays only after singles show skill.
  2. Correlation caps prevent lucky stacks.
  3. Injury filter removes bets damaged by backup replacements (2nd/3rd/4th string).
  4. Paper-only for 4–8 weeks; real allocation only after gate passage.
  5. Weekly review: strategies decaying below the edge threshold are removed.
