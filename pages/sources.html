Source URL conventions used on this site

  • Injury report by week: https://www.nfl.com/injuries/league/{YEAR}/REG{WEEK} — example: 2026 Week 10
  • Team roster: https://www.nfl.com/teams/{team-slug}/ — example: Kansas City Chiefs

Non-official / third-party sources (flagged)

Depth charts listing 1st/2nd/3rd/4th-string backups are not published as a single official league page on NFL.com. Third-party depth-chart aggregators observed in search results (e.g., nfl-depth-charts.com, sticktothemodel.com, tools.thehuddle.com) are NOT official NFL sources. Any depth-chart replacement-string value used on this site is flagged unverified and must be re-checked against official NFL team roster pages before use.

Betting lines & odds

The NFL does not publish official betting lines or odds. All lines, signals, parlay/prop constructions, validation gates, and correlation values in this project are original analytical work product, not official NFL data. They are labeled "analysis — not official source" throughout the site.

Verification rule: if a value can't be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged as unverified. Never infer or fill in values.
