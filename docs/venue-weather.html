Venue, roof, surface and the NWS overlay

The finding that decides this page. The official NFL.com game-centre page publishes, in its “Game Info” block, exactly two venue fields: Location (city, state or country) and Stadium (name). It publishes no roof state and no playing surface, on any of the thirteen Week 11 pages read 2026-11-18. Roof and surface are therefore not obtainable from the official sources this project is restricted to, and every roof and surface cell below is blank and flagged rather than filled from general knowledge about the building. A total model that consumed a remembered roof value could never be audited against a source.

How this could be finished, and what blocks it. A full league overlay needs a published coordinate pair per stadium and a roof/surface register. Neither is in the official source read here. Two honest routes: read the stadium page for each club (a different source class, currently outside the allow-list and therefore a decision for the owner), or license a weather/env data feed. Until one of those happens the cells stay blank, and the page keeps its one worked NWS example as proof the mechanism works.
