Depth of the replacement

The NFL's weekly injury report says that a player is out. It does not say how far down the roster the replacement comes from, and that is the number that matters: a second-string replacement and a fourth-string replacement are not the same event. The only official-domain source carrying the order is the depth chart each club publishes on its own website, and every one of those charts is self-labelled unofficial — compiled by club communications staff or club writers — so each value below inherits that caveat (F06).

String of the absent player, and of the man behind him

Read the columns together: the string the absent player held, the player the club chart lists next, the string that player holds, and how many players the chart still lists behind him. Where the chart names nobody — the absent player is the last name at that spot, or he is not on the chart at all — the field is left blank and marked unverified rather than guessed. The last two columns catch a compounded loss: a replacement who is himself on the injury report.

How this register fits the other one

This repository holds two independent replacement-depth registers. They were built in parallel by different sessions from the same kind of source. Together they cover 15 of 32 clubs:

  • This register (data/depth_replacement.csv): DAL, DET, GB, NE, NO, TEN — every Out/Doubtful player on the Week 10 report who appears on that club's chart, 33 rows.
  • The other register (data/replacement_depth_week10_2026.csv, see Deficiency tracking): SEA, MIA, BAL, CLE, AZ, NYG — a quarterback-centred set with the chart order quoted verbatim.
  • One direct disagreement, recorded rather than resolved: this session's request to www.seattleseahawks.com returned a consent and advertising interstitial, while the other session retrieved the Seattle chart from www.seahawks.com. Seattle is therefore absent here and present there. Nothing was inferred to bridge the gap.
  • One compounded loss the two registers agree on the shape of: Dallas safety P.J. Locke is Out and the man behind him, Juanyeh Thomas, is himself Doubtful — a second-string replacement who may not play.

Coverage, and why it stops where it stops

Six clubs publish a chart that could be read as text on 2026-11-14: Detroit, New England, Dallas, Green Bay (chart dated Nov. 10, 2026), Tennessee (chart dated 11/10/26) and New Orleans.

  • Houston publishes its chart as an image, so no ordinal value can be read from the page text.
  • Indianapolis and Minnesota could not be retrieved on this pass, and Seattle returned a consent interstitial at the address used here. None was guessed.
  • Two players on this page — Kerby Joseph (DET) and Micah Parsons (GB) — are listed Out or Doubtful on the league report but do not appear on their own club's chart at all. Their rows are blank and flagged.
