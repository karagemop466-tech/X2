Offense vs defence matchup matrix, 1st to 4th string

The brief asks for one screen that shows a trench or coverage mismatch — a WR1 against a promoted third-string corner, an EDGE1 against a backup tackle — and asks, separately, that injuries be tracked as a surprise and that depth be modelled from official chart order rather than jersey number, experience or salary. This page is the join of those three requirements, and it is deliberately narrower than it is wide: it publishes a string matrix for the clubs whose own site publishes a machine-readable chart that this session actually read, and it leaves every other club blank with the reason stated.

1. Read this before the table

  • No points are estimated. A "points lost to injury" column would be an invented number. The brief's own requirement is an ablation — the same model with and without personnel features, reported with uncertainty — and this model carries no personnel feature at all, so the ablation cannot yet be run. What is published instead is the structural fact: who the club itself names at each string, and which of those players the official report designates Out or Doubtful.
  • Blank is not healthy. The official report's game-status cell is blank for 176 of 347 listed players. A blank cell here means "the report does not say", never "available". Substituting a level for a missing value is exactly what R60 forbids.
  • The report is the Week 10 report. nfl.com/injuries renders Week 10 on 2026-11-18 despite a week=11 URL parameter (F94), so the designation column is labelled Week 10 and the Week 11 designation is blank by design. The 2026-11-18 club pages are the only Week 11 evidence on this page.
  • A shared cell stays shared. Club charts put two players in one cell (8 rows here). Picking one to be "the starter" would fabricate a starter the club never named. The column shows both, and slot_type says shared.
  • An empty cell stays empty. 200 rows carry a blank cell where the club published nothing. Nothing is renumbered to fill the gap.
  • 22 clubs are not in this table. One (HOU) publishes its chart as an image and is refused rather than transcribed by eye; the rest were not read in this session. Both are counted in the coverage table below so the gap cannot be mistaken for "no deficiencies".

2. Per-club deficiency summary

"Published player slots" counts every player the club's own chart names in slots 1–4; "designated" counts only those the official report marks Out or Doubtful. A player on the report but absent from the chart, or on the chart but absent from the report, is excluded from both — not guessed into a slot. GB is on bye in Week 11, so its matchup cell is blank.
