The findings this pass added

  1. Home-team bias in the originated side (F76). Not statistically significant, but the direction is consistent and it is now measured rather than assumed.
  2. The neutral-site defect, half closed. Week 11 MIN at SF is published with the home-field term dropped and the schedule column that says why; the 137 completed historical rows still carry a blank neutral_site value, which is the open half of F71 and is not resolved here.
  3. The constant reference total explained and re-derived. It is the league mean game total — 45.9197 points over the 137-game ledger, published as 45.9 — not a game-specific projection.
  4. One wording defect confirmed fixed. The ledger is weeks 1-9 complete plus the Week 10 Thursday game, not "weeks 1-10"; the label no longer appears anywhere in the repository.
