## Reading the status

- **Open** — the required information is not yet available from a confirmed source;
  a decision cannot be made yet. A suggestion to discover the information is
  provided on the [Suggestions](../suggestions/) page.
- **Partially answered** — the official source is confirmed but a machine-readable
  endpoint, update cadence, or specific value is still unresolved.
- Where the **current answer** references a value that is a project decision rather
  than an official fact, that value is intentionally left blank and flagged rather
  than guessed.

## What is already known (and verified)

- Official NFL data sources are confirmed and linked on the
  [Data Sources](../data-sources/) page: standings, injury report, team stats,
  transactions, and the rulebook.
- CLV is the correct skill metric for removing luck; this is established methodology
  and is the basis for the validation gates.
- Injury and depth data are verifiable today: real, current official injury reports
  and depth charts are already reflected on the
  [Injuries & Depth](../injuries-and-depth/) page.

## What is not yet known (and must not be guessed)

- The exact CLV threshold, minimum sample size, and correlation bounds — these are
  pre-declared project decisions, not official facts, and are left open.
- Live line values and live performance — no feed is connected, so these are blank
  and flagged.
