Decision gates — the questions that must be answered before any path is chosen

This is the review page you asked for. Each row is a question that must be answered before a route can sensibly be chosen, what is known now with a source, how to find the missing answer, and the choice you will be facing. The Decision column is yours: nothing is chosen automatically, and no route is implemented while its gate is open. Gates are ordered, not equal: DQ-01 (data rights) and DQ-02 (jurisdiction and contest design) block everything downstream.
