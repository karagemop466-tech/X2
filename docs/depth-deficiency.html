Deficiency tracking — units, starters and officially named replacements

What is verified and what is not. The injury report states who is Out, Doubtful or Questionable and what position they play. It does not state whether that player is a first, second, third or fourth string player. NFL.com's own team depth-chart pages return 404. Club websites do publish charts — detroitlions.com and patriots.com were located and read — but each page labels its own chart "unofficial" (compiled by club communications or by club writers), so these are club publications, not league depth charts, and are not treated as authoritative. The string-rank column below is therefore left unverified rather than guessed (Flag F06); where a club chart is cited, the caveat travels with it (Flag F18). Any statement of the form "the backup must now start" is made only where an official NFL.com report names the replacement.
