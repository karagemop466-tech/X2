Results register v2 — the completed 2026 results, beside the published one

The site's published backtest has always run on 137 games: weeks 1–9 plus the Week 10 Thursday game. Thirteen further Week 10 games had been settled, transcribed and reconciled but never ingested (F96, F105), and the eight completed international games carried no neutral-site flag anywhere in the corpus (F71). This page publishes the completed register — 150 games, 13 of them spliced, 8 flagged neutral — as a parallel, versioned register of results. The 137-game files stay exactly as they are, and every published metric on the backtest and leaderboard pages still refers to them.

How this relates to the pre-registered corpus. Both registers were built on 2026-11-18 from the same 13 settled games, by different sessions, for different purposes: that page holds model output (89 reproduced rows plus 13 new, each with its probability and its fit inputs), this one holds results and venue facts plus what re-adoption would cost. They reconcile: the 5-of-13 holdout count published there is reproduced exactly by the unflagged fit here, and the 2-row difference between the two is the neutral-site treatment alone (F119).

Why a parallel register and not a correction

  • A re-run changes published numbers, so it must be a version. The same method on the completed corpus moves the headline side hit rate against the naive reference from 0.652 to 0.578 and the count of bets that clear the pre-registered threshold from 50 to 59. Overwriting the corpus in place would have republished those figures with no trace of the change.
  • The prospective forecast log is sealed. data/prospective_forecast_log.sha256 carries a SHA-256 over the pre-kickoff Week 11 lines. Any re-fit would have produced a different seal (5aaf29db31d18129… vs b32752af2aa7dc5e…) — which is exactly why a re-registration has to be announced as one and not performed as a data-capture side effect.
  • The choice stays with the owner. The Review console records no decision, because a decision recorded by an agent would destroy what the field means; this session recorded none. What it did instead is supply the missing measurement so P09 can be chosen with its cost visible.
  • Nothing unverified entered the register. Every one of the 13 added rows came from the official by-week page — scores transcribed, never computed from win–loss records — and each row carries the minute it was read.
