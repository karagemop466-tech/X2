The blind spot, stated as a number

58 of 65 named risks have no official source establishing the player's role, and therefore no replacement depth rank. That is not a presentation problem: it means an injury adjustment cannot currently be priced, and any page that claims otherwise is guessing. The cause is F06 — the NFL publishes no league-level depth chart, and the club charts that do exist self-label "unofficial" (F45).
