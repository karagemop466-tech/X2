The user's requirements, mapped to gates

  • "Each strategy must pass validation gates." That is EG0 through EG9 for every strategy, plus one family gate.
  • "Determine correlation values." Correlation is gated by EG12 and EG13, which cannot pass without paired settled data. See Correlation plan.
  • "Remove luck from the equation." Luck cannot be removed. It can be bounded and disclosed: a bootstrap interval, a search log and a held-out block. See Anti-luck for why "remove" is the wrong target and what replaces it.
  • "Build strategies that are selectively skill based." A strategy is selectively applied only where the gate ledger shows measured skill; until then the correct selective rule is to apply nothing.
  • "Rewards for top performing strategies." EG16 blocks any reward until a qualified legal review covers the jurisdiction and the contest design.
