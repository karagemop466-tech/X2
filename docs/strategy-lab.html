Strategy lab

This page holds the qualitative framework. Historical quantitative research lives on the Backtest, Validation gates, Correlations and Parlays pages. The prospective Leaderboard is intentionally unranked because strategy registration and prospective grading are not complete. The research framework taken over in T52 lives on Signals, Skill engine, Anti-luck, Correlation plan, Starters & depth and Repo review. The inputs needed (licensed odds, historical outcomes, verified role evidence and paired correlation data) are not available here. No validation can eliminate football randomness or guarantee a result; out-of-sample testing can reduce hindsight and quantify uncertainty, not remove variance. This is a research plan, not betting advice. Numeric thresholds are intentionally blank: general statistical guidance does not supply an NFL-specific sample size, return cutoff, significance level or paper-trading duration. Independent line framework (proposed; no values estimated)

Originate the forecast from pregame NFL performance and verified availability, not by copying a sportsbook price.
