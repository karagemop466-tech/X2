Independent cross-check of the line model

Why this page exists. While this session was running, a second session (73cbec41) independently transcribed the same 2026 season and built its own line model, and that work is now on main. Rather than publish two competing leaderboards, this page reports what happens when the two independent implementations are compared. Where they agree, confidence rises. Where they disagree, the difference is stated instead of being smoothed over.

The one material disagreement

The other implementation reports 0 of 7 strategies as qualified and proposes reward tiers. This session's independent scoring of 8 competing rules on 96 unseen games finds 0 qualified once the multiple-comparison adjustment is applied: testing several rules and keeping the best one manufactures significance, and the leading rules' intervals no longer clear zero once that is charged for. The recommendation on this page is that no reward tier be offered or paid until that disagreement is resolved by a pre-registered test on a larger sample. See NIST on multiple comparisons ↗ and Flag F42.
