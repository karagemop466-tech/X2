Season results — the verified results feed

This table is the project's verified results feed (requirement R38): data/games_2026_results.csv, all 137 games of 2026 played through the Week 10 Thursday game (weeks 1-9 complete, plus the Week 10 Thursday game), transcribed from the official NFL.com by-week schedule pages and read 2026-11-14. Every row links to the official schedule and the official game page. Verification: the wins, losses, ties, points for and points against implied by this feed were compared with the official 2026 standings for all 32 teams and match exactly on every column; a second, independent transcription of the same season agrees on every score (cross-check).

What the feed makes computable, and what it does not. Backtests and forecast-skill scores are computed from this feed — that is what the Backtest and Leaderboard pages show. Return on investment cannot be computed at all until the price-snapshot store holds real market numbers: ROI needs a price, and no licensed price source is connected (Flags F07, F32; requirement R37). No ROI value is ever estimated here.

Canonical verified results feed

data/results_feed_2026.csv (session f25d6b55, task T52, advances requirement R38) is the single canonical feed this project grades everything against: the backtest, the bets, the gates, the correlations and the price-snapshot store all consume exactly these 137 rows and nothing else. Every row carries its verification status (aggregates reproduce the official standings exactly — V01 and RF07), the date verified, the check register it was validated against, and links to the official game page and schedule page. ROI itself is still not computed anywhere, because no authorised price source exists (Flag F07); the feed makes ROI computable the moment a permitted price is connected.
