The key findings, what is verified, and what is not yet known.

Review path

A single ordered route through the open decisions, so one reviewer can choose a direction before anything is built. Start here to decide; use the other pages to check the evidence.

Decision path

The ten questions that must be answered, in order, before any dependent choice is made.

Model & Week 11 lines

Our own originated spreads, totals and moneylines for Week 11 — originated side first, then total, then moneyline — with the fair line, market line and difference as separate columns.

Price snapshots

The price-snapshot store: opening and closing numbers for every market originated. Values are blank and flagged — no licensed odds feed exists (F51).

Originated lines

Side first, then total, then moneyline: our own fair numbers, the reference line and the edge, plus our opening and current number for every market.

Backtest

All 89 walk-forward out-of-sample predictions against what actually happened, game by game.

Leaderboard

Seven strategies ranked on signal (forecast skill) only — staking is kept separate so luck cannot mask a bad signal — with gates and required sample sizes.

Validation gates

Nine gates, what each one tests, and which strategies pass — including the demotion gate that sends a live strategy back to paper.

Correlations

Nine correlation values computed from official results, with intervals and p-values.

Correlation provenance

Every correlation traced to its inputs, the official page behind them and the n it was computed on.

Parlays

Same-game two-leg tickets: observed joint hit rate against the independence shortcut.

Market tables

Price-snapshot store (opening and closing, blank until a source exists), fair vs market lines, signal vs staking, and the demotion gate.

Market sheet

Every Week 11 market in origination order, with the fair line, the market line and the difference kept in three separate columns, plus the sealed pre-kickoff forecast log.

Player props

Which prop outcomes are officially obtainable, which are not held here, and the same-game correlation matrix with n on every value.

Event risk

Named players, official status words and the exact market each one can invalidate, plus the count of risks whose role is not officially confirmed.

Review console

The decision section: eight proposals, three options each, a recommendation, the file each would change, and a recorded-choice mechanism that ships empty.

Season results

The verified results feed: all 137 transcribed 2026 games, each reconciled against the official standings — what backtests run on, and why ROI still cannot be computed.

Results feed

The settlement view: the official final score plus how the side, the total and the moneyline we originated actually settled.

Injuries

All 345 Week 10 injury report rows with source links, plus NFL.com news headlines.

Games

The 14 Week 10 matchups with records and broadcast windows, all from NFL.com.

Depth exposure

Out and Doubtful players by unit, showing where a backup may be needed. Starter order is unverified.

Strategy lab

The hypotheses, the gates and the reward framework. Strategies are graded on signal; the reward tiers are proposed and not offered (R36), and nothing is paid.

Questions

What we know, what we do not, and how to find the missing information.

Requirements

Each requirement from the brief with its current status.

Suggestions

Review proposals before future implementation, with a recommended path and the gate that blocks each one.

Verification

What was checked line by line, against which official page, and what the result was.

Flagged for review

Irregularities, unverified items and missing official data, each with a source link.

Sources

Every official source used, with what it supports.

Depth of replacement

How far down the roster the replacement comes from: 2nd, 3rd or 4th string, read from official club depth charts.

Repository audit

Twenty-two checks run against live official pages and recomputations, with the result of each one.

Independent cross-check

A second independent transcription and model compared against the first: what agrees, and the one thing that does not.

Deficiency tracking

Unit exposure by side of ball, officially named starters and replacements, and the depth of each replacement (2nd/3rd/4th string where a chart verifies it).
