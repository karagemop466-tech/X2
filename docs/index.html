NFL Research Dashboard

A source-linked research dashboard for NFL availability, starter identity, independent-line signals, validation gates, anti-luck testing, correlation planning and the decisions still standing in the way. Every table row links to an official NFL, government or statistical source. Unconfirmed values are blank, never estimated.

What this site will not tell you: it will not give you a pick, a line or a price. No strategy here has been validated, and no reward is enabled. The evidence for both statements is on every page.
