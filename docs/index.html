Freshness and what moved since the last capture (2026-11-18)

The site's own data is dated. The published injury table is the 2026-11-14 read; the results feed stops after one Week 10 game; and the model's Week 11 line is fitted on 137 of the 150 games the official standings now show. A second dated read was taken today, and it is the first comparison the repository has ever had between two captures of the same official page.

  • Injury hub: 345 published rows vs 347 rows read today; 19 players changed between the two reads, in 4 of 28 club blocks. Captures & deltas.
  • Results feed: 13 settled Week 10 games are absent from the corpus the backtests grade on; adding the transcribed rows reconciles all 32 clubs. Feed completeness.
  • Forecast outputs: every model line is now stored with its inputs and its probabilities, and reproduces from the published CSVs exactly. Forecast records.
  • Cadence hazard (new): https://www.nfl.com/injuries/?season=2026&week=11&type=REG rendered the Week 10 tables today, and the club records beside them had already moved to post-Week-10 values. A scheduled capture that trusts the URL would silently store a stale week and a live standings level. Any capture job must record the rendered week label and pin it.
