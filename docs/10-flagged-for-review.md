# Flagged for Review

Irregularities, discrepancies, and unverified items that need review. **These do
not block the site or the merge.** They are published here, with their source links,
so they can be reviewed after the site is live and changes can be requested.

Nothing on this site is inferred. Items that cannot be confirmed from an official
source are left blank and flagged here rather than filled in.

## How to resolve an item

1. Open the linked source(s) for the item.
2. Confirm or correct the value against the official source.
3. Update the corresponding data file and rebuild (`npm run build`).
4. Remove the item from this register (or update its status) once confirmed.

Items F-02, F-03, and F-04 are **unverified values** — they are left blank by
design until the required data feed is connected. Items F-01 and F-05 are
**irregularities** — a cross-source discrepancy and a source-type caveat — that
should be reviewed and, where possible, resolved through a documented rule. Item
F-06 is a **missing feed** to be confirmed before player-prop signals are fully
sourced.
