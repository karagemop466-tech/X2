Results feed — settled markets

One row per decided game holding the official final score and how each market we originate settled. This is the companion to Season results: the same 137-game feed, with the settlement columns a backtest, a closing-line-value study or a return-on-investment figure would be computed from. This is not a complete current-season corpus: the 2026-11-18 standings imply 13 settled Week 10 games that are absent here. Every score comes from the official NFL.com by-week schedule pages. All 32 teams reconcile against the official standings as read on 2026-11-14, which is a check of arithmetic and not of currency: against the current read 26 of 32 clubs do not reconcile (V35, flag F113). The 13 games are transcribed and source-linked on the Feed completeness page and scored out of sample on the Pre-registered corpus page, but they are not spliced into this frozen feed (P09). The settlement columns are computed from that score and the walk-forward line scripts/model.py actually published for the game, so they are settlement against our own line, not ROI or a market result: the market columns are blank because there is no licensed odds feed, so no price, no stake and no return (F07).

Settlement conventions

  • Side. The published number is the home team's predicted margin. The home side covers when the actual home margin is strictly greater, loses when strictly less, and pushes when exactly equal.
  • Total. The over wins when actual total points are strictly greater than the published total, the under when strictly less, push when equal.
  • Moneyline. The home side wins when the actual home margin is strictly positive. The ledger contains one tie (Giants 27, Commanders 27, week 5), which a two-way moneyline would settle as a push.
