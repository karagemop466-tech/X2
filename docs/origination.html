1. The order, and what it does and does not buy

Side first, then the total, then the moneyline. The moneyline is derived from stage 1, so it can never contradict the side published before it. The side and the total are two readings of one fitted system: a publication order is not two independent confirmations — CR05 measured the pair at r = -0.036 (n = 89), which is exactly what lets them disagree.
