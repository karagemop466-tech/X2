Line book

Opening and closing numbers for the markets this project originates. The opening number of our own line is a fact about our own publication — the timestamp of the commit that first published it — so it is recorded here and never estimated. No bookmaker price appears on this page or in this file: the project has no authorised odds source (Flag F07), and NFL.com's terms prohibit systematic retrieval of odds to compile a database absent written consent (Flag F16). The market-price columns exist so the shape is fixed now and the cell can be filled only from a source that is authorised — they are blank in all 78 rows.

1. What is stored, and what may never be back-filled

The same contract governs the store and the originated view (Origination), so the two cannot drift apart.

Every snapshot is timed against the official kickoff for its game. A line first published after kickoff is excluded rather than recorded as an opening number, so the ledger cannot quietly fill itself with retrospective numbers. Re-check with python3 scripts/line_book.py --check.

2. The ledger

One row per market per snapshot. Model fair line is our published number. Reference line is the naive benchmark this project grades against and is labelled as such — it is not a market price (Flag F32). Kickoff dates come from the official Week 11 schedule and the Week 10 schedule. Provenance names the commit that published the line; the history is read from the repository, and this sandbox clone may be shallow (Flag F60).
