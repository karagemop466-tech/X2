Parallel v2 research corpus — decision P09 = option B

The published record is v1: 137 games, frozen. Decision P09 asked whether to adopt the 13 official Week 10 finals and the settled international neutral-site flags into it. The recorded answer is option B — the console's own recommendation: publish the adoption as a parallel research corpus and leave every published number alone. Nothing on any other page of this site changed because of this decision.
