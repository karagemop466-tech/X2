Parlays and player-prop combinations

A parlay's price depends on the joint probability of its legs, and the joint probability is not the product of the legs unless the legs are independent. This page measures that directly on the only paired legs this repository has: a same-game side and total, each taken when the model and the reference line disagree by at least 3.0 points.

Reading the result

  • The two legs did not hit together as often as independence implies (0.208 observed against 0.278 expected), and the phi correlation is negative (-0.299). Directionally, that means pricing this ticket as if the legs were independent would have been generous to the ticket buyer.
  • It is 24 tickets. The 95% interval on phi runs from -0.63 to +0.12 and the p-value is 0.16, so this is a warning sign, not a measurement. It fails the minimum-sample gate and is not qualified on the leaderboard.
  • Player-prop combinations are not available. This repository holds no player-stat outcomes, no prop thresholds, no prices and no settlement rules, so no prop or prop-combo correlation can be computed. Those cells are blank by design (Flag F31).
