Read the Recommended column, then record your choice. Nothing here is implemented: no option is switched on until you choose it and its gate is answered on the Decision gates page. Where a recommendation depends on an open gate, the gap is named, so the choice is informed rather than optimistic.

The three that matter most

  1. SR-02 — settle the data rights. Everything else is downstream of permission. Until DQ-01 is answered the site should stay a source-linked summary.
  2. SR-03 — adopt the official gamebook. This is the one requirement that turned out to be solvable from an official source: starter identity is published, just not as a pregame depth chart.
  3. SR-09 — make the anti-luck suite a hard gate. Without it the leaderboard rewards luck, which is the opposite of what you asked for.
