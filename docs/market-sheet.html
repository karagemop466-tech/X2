The fair line is computed; the market columns are blank and marked UNVERIFIED, because no licensed odds feed is connected (F07). A blank cell here is the correct output, not a missing value.

Fair line, fair price, and the market columns

The fair price is the American price implied by the model — the price at which a break-even book would offer the bet (prices are derived as 1/p; see scripts/desk_v2.py). It is not a market price and must never be read as one. The model's probability of covering its own originated number is one half by construction, so side and total show +100.

Why the market column stays blank

What would fill it

  • A licensed historical odds archive with written model-use rights (DEC-01 / P01).
  • A timestamped opening number and a timestamped closing number for the same market.
  • A settlement rule for each market, so a push can be told from a win.

What will not fill it

  • A public sportsbook page: not an official source, and its terms are unreviewed.
  • An NFL.com odds widget: it displays partner prices live, but it is not an archive and the FAQ does not grant reuse rights (F07).
  • Any estimate, midpoint or "typical" number. Nothing is inferred here.
