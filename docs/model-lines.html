Week 11 lines — originated in order: side, then total, then moneyline

Each market is originated in a fixed order: the side (spread) first, then the total, then the moneyline. For every market the model's fair line, the market line and the difference are published as separate columns. The market columns are deliberately blank: this project has no licensed odds feed, so no market price can be confirmed from an official source and the difference against the market cannot be computed (Flags F07, F31). The fair moneyline is the no-vig American price implied by the model's win probability, not a sportsbook price. Spread is from the home team's perspective; P(home) uses the model's out-of-sample residual spread of 12.25 points and treats a tie as half a win. Calibration: actual margin on predicted margin has slope 0.885 (SE 0.28; corrected 95% CI 0.328 to 1.443, p 0.683 against slope 1.0 — C09 correction, F48, session f25d6b55). The reference total is the same constant for every Week 11 game by construction: 2 × the league-average points per team per game over games strictly before the reference week (F44 resolved by documentation, session f25d6b55).

Reference total derivation (Flag F44, resolved). The reference total is 45.9 in every row by construction: scripts/model.py computes it as 2 × the league average points per team per game from the games strictly before the week — here all 137 games of the verified results feed. Recomputed from that official dataset on 2026-11-14: 6,291 total points over 137 games = 22.96 points per team per game; 2 × 22.96 = 45.92, which rounds to 45.9. It is the naive league-average-total benchmark the totals strategy is graded against, not a per-game forecast. The reference spread is not constant — it is 2.5 (fixed home field) plus each team's season scoring differential per game.

Team ratings

Ratings are in points per game relative to a league-average team. Attack: positive means the team scores more than average. Defence: positive means it allows fewer than average. Net rating is attack plus defence, which is what drives the spread. Ratings are shrunk toward zero, so extreme records are deliberately pulled back.

Method, and what it deliberately leaves out

  • Fit. One ridge-regularised least-squares system: points scored = league mean + attack rating − opponent defence rating + home-field term, fitted on every team-game to date. Spread = (attack + defence) difference plus home field; total = both attacks minus both defences plus home field.
  • No look-ahead. Every out-of-sample prediction was fitted only on games played strictly before that week. This is enforced in code and computed as gate G1: all 10 no-look-ahead tests passed across 10 weeks, with the audit recorded in data/gate_results.csv (Flag F50 resolved).
  • Not included: play-by-play, yards per play, turnovers, personnel, participation, weather, travel, rest, coaching, and injuries. One injury snapshot cannot be backtested, so no injury adjustment is applied (Flags F06, F34).
  • Reproducibility. python3 scripts/model.py regenerates every number on this page from data/games_2026_results.csv. Fixed random seed 20261114 for the bootstrap and permutation tests.
  • Staleness. These lines were fitted before Week 10's Sunday games were played. Re-run the model script once Week 10 finishes (Flag F36).
