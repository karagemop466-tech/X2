Independent lines — our own numbers, not a sportsbook's

What this is. A projection produced only from official NFL results: every game played earlier in the season, refit week by week. No sportsbook price, odds feed or market line is read, copied or implied by any number here. The two market columns are deliberately empty because no licensed odds source is connected to this repository (Flag F07). These are research projections, not betting lines and not betting advice.

How the projection is built. Each team carries a scoring rate (points scored per game) and an allowance rate (points allowed per game), shrunk toward the league average. A team's expected score is the league average plus half its own scoring edge plus half the opponent's allowance edge; a home-field term estimated from earlier weeks is split evenly between the two sides. The Week 10 numbers below are fitted on weeks 1 to 9 only. The same rule family scores 0.0767 skill against a zero-information baseline over 96 out-of-sample games.

What these numbers can and cannot tell you

  • They can tell you which side the official results favour, by how much, and how uncertain that is.
  • They cannot tell you whether a price is good. Without an authorised odds history there is no closing-line value, no expected value per unit staked, and no claim of profit. Any such claim would be unsupported.
  • Totals are weak. Across the out-of-sample window the best total projection missed by 10.994 points per game against 11.040 for simply projecting the league average every game — a gap of 0.05 points, which is noise — and its correlation with the actual total (r = 0.0641) is not distinguishable from zero.
  • Availability is not in the number. The projection is fitted on results only. Injuries and confirmed replacements are reported separately on the deficiency page and are deliberately not folded into the line, because no back-test of an injury adjustment is possible here (Flag F20).
