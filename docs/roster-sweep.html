The 32-team roster / reserve-list sweep

What this sweep answers, and what it cannot. The brief asks for a 32-team roster/IR sweep before any team-level model is trusted, and gives three worked examples. The sweep joins three official surfaces rather than reading one: the official NFL.com reserve-list feed (Reserve/Injured transactions, month by month), the published Week 10 injury report, and the per-player status column on each club’s NFL.com roster page (ACT, RES, RSR, PUP, CUT, DEV, TRC, TRD, TRT).

The key point the sweep exists to enforce: the weekly injury report is a designation list, not an availability list. A player absent from it may be on Reserve/Injured, on a practice squad, suspended or simply unlisted — so “not listed” must never be read as “available”. That is why the reserve list is captured separately.

The brief’s three worked examples, checked rather than assumed

  • Baltimore, “still publishes Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley at QB”. Confirmed as a listing, corrected as an implication: the BAL roster page read 2026-11-18 lists Lamar Jackson, RES and Tyler Huntley, RES — both on the reserve list — beside Jacoby Brissett, ACT, the quarterback the club traded for. So the club does still show both quarterbacks, but with a status that says they are not available. A reader who took the names without the status column would draw exactly the wrong conclusion; the status column is the fact.
  • Malik Willis, “absent from the weekly report because he is on injured reserve”. Confirmed directly: the MIA roster page read 2026-11-18 shows Malik Willis, RES, and the reserve-list feed records his Reserve/Injured transaction on 11/10. Absence from the report is not health.
  • Seattle and Cincinnati “shown as injury-free”. Not reproduced on this read: the published Week 10 register lists players for both clubs. Recorded as a resolved transcription issue rather than a live conflict (see the injuries page).

Coverage, stated plainly

The reserve-list feed gave 94 Reserve/Injured transactions across 31 of 32 clubs in the August–November pages. NYJ has no Reserve/Injured transaction in those pages; its row carries zero and is not imputed. Full roster-page reads were taken for 3 clubs (BAL, MIA, HOU); every other club’s row is blank and flagged “unchecked” rather than assumed consistent. That is the honest state: the reserve list covers the league, the page cross-check does not yet.

A correction on this session’s own work, recorded because it matters. The first pass transcribed only the first rendered chunk of each roster page (the renderer splits a roster across three) and concluded that reserve-list players were missing from the pages. Re-reading the later chunks showed the pages do list them with a non-ACT status. The half-read rows were discarded and the pages re-transcribed in full; scripts/roster_sweep.py carries a CORRECTIONS entry that fails the run if a corrected cell ever stops matching. The one genuine disagreement the corrected read found (a Texans linebacker the page shows ACT while the reserve list placed him on Reserve/Injured) is kept as a finding rather than overwritten into agreement.
