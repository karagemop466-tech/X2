Signal and staking remain separate

When prospective evidence is sufficient, the board will rank signal by the pre-registered proper-score metric with game-clustered uncertainty and a multiplicity adjustment. Staking results will be displayed separately and cannot affect signal rank. ROI remains blank until authorized opening/closing prices and settlement rules exist.

Entry and promotion gates

Pre-registration, an append-only forecast freeze, official outcome verification, sample sufficiency, calibration and benchmark-relative skill, multiplicity control, robustness, valid price/joint evidence where applicable, data rights and jurisdictional review are required. Parlays and prop combos remain excluded without directly validated joint probabilities and applicable push/void rules. See Validation Gates and Decision Path.

Demotion gate — live back to paper

G9 is defined but inert because there are no live strategies. Any future live strategy must return to paper if its approved rolling evidence window fails an earlier gate, the pre-registered skill interval no longer supports skill, the forecast record or source provenance fails validation, or legal/data-rights clearance lapses. Numeric cut-offs are not activated while they remain unapproved (F17).
