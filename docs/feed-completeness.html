Results-feed completeness

The feed every backtest and every ROI number depends on holds 137 games. Today's official standings imply 150 completed games through Week 10 (149 wins, 149 losses, 2 ties = 300 team-games). The gap is the Week 10 slate: the feed carries the Thursday, Nov 12 game and none of the 13 that followed it, although data/games_week10_2026.csv lists all 14 matchups with the result column blank on 13 of them.

Nothing on this page changes a published metric. The 13 scores are transcribed from the official Week 10 schedule page and then checked against the official standings: with the 13 rows added, every club's win-loss-tie record, points for and points against equals the standings exactly. Without them, 26 of 32 clubs do not. Two independent official surfaces agreeing to the point is the verification.

The fitted home-field term on the published fit is negative (−0.4 pts), which is why "no home edge at a neutral venue" moves some games the other way.
