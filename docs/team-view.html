Per-team view: Week 10, 2026

One row per club (32). Injury counts come from the official NFL.com Week 10 injury report, as held in data/injuries_week10_2026.csv; the rows reconcile to 66 Out, 10 Doubtful and 84 Questionable (345 listed players, of which 185 have no game designation). Four clubs were on bye and have no injury section (official schedule, Teams on Bye: CHI, DEN, PHI, TB); they are shown blank, never as injury-free.

Starter order is known for only 6 of 32 clubs: the club team-site charts in data/depth_replacement.csv. For every other club the starter column is blank, not inferred. Named replacements shown: 16 verified (player, replacement) pairs from club charts, plus the article-named rows. A player with no verified replacement stays unnamed. Two charts (DET, NE) label themselves unofficial (F05, F18); no chart in the register states its as-of date (chart_as_of is not stated on any page).

Verification scope (read 2026-11-15 from https://www.nfl.com/injuries/): WAS, NYG, NE and DET were compared row by row (player, injury, practice and game status) and match the repo file. ATL, DAL, GB, NO, SEA and TEN were compared on their club totals and on every starter row, and match. KC and the other clubs were not re-checked in this session: their counts are as in the repo file and are not independently verified (F89). A named replacement who is also on the report is labelled with that status.

Open irregularity (F83): the repo injury file holds 345 Week 10 rows, while the brief records 279 live rows. This page reports the file as it stands and does not choose between the two figures. Flag F89 records the per-team dependency.
